Some thoughts take 600 words. LinkedIn gives you 250.

For the past few weeks I've been posting about AI — specifically about Co-Intelligence, Ethan Mollick's book on what it means to work with AI deliberately rather than just use it. The posts do what short-form is good for: name a concept, make one point, leave a question.

But there are angles I keep not having room for. The synthesis. The personal. The original.

So I started a newsletter.

Noesis Letters — reading carefully, writing further.
Most weeks, 500–600 words, no bullet points. Each letter takes one idea further than a post can.

The first letter is already live: why learning gets harder as AI gets better — not easier. The second lands tomorrow.

If that sounds like something you'd want in your inbox, the link is in the first comment.
