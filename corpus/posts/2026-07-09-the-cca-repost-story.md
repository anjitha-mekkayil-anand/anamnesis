Three months ago I reposted someone else's excitement about a new certification — Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect — and added my own line underneath: "Let's see where this goes."

I built a six-week study plan around it. Nine hours a week, mapped domain by domain: agentic architecture, tool design, Claude Code workflows, context management. A self-gate of 850 out of 1000 on the practice exam before I'd even book the real one. Twelve weeks of buffer, more than double what the material needed.

In May, I found the actual eligibility requirement, past the excitement everyone was reposting: the certification is only open to people at Anthropic's partner companies — not something the announcement mentioned at all.

The study plan aimed at something that was never open to me in the first place. What stayed was the six weeks of structure — the same hours went straight into study time for AI-103, the certification that's actually open to me.

How often do you check eligibility before you build the whole plan?
