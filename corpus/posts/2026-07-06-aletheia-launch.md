About two and a half months ago I wrote a file explaining why I was building a personal wiki maintained entirely by an AI — the architecture, the reasoning, why it beats search. I wrote it just to keep my own agent consistent between sessions.

Today I reread it and the file read like a pitch, almost word for word.

The idea is simple: instead of an AI retrieving chunks from your documents every time you ask a question, it maintains a living wiki that compounds. Every source it reads updates related pages, flags contradictions, builds cross-references. Each new source makes the whole wiki richer.

I stripped my own content out, kept the pattern, and published it as Aletheia — link in the first comment. A template for anyone who wants to build the same thing for themselves.

The name roughly translates to "un-forgetting." Most personal wikis die when the discipline of maintaining them runs out. Here, the agent maintains it — every session, automatically.
